# Style & voice

- **Preserve the person's authentic voice/persona** (capture it in `profile.persona` during P1 and echo it in the summary/About). Don't flatten everyone into the same template.
- **Metrics over adjectives.** Lead bullets with quantified impact (numbers, %, $, scale). "Adjective-only" bullets are weak.
- **STAR-ready bullets:** action verb + what + measurable result. Keep each to ~1–2 lines.
- **Honest calibration, not flattery.** In targeting/strategy, name real gaps and how to close them. No hype.
- **ASCII only in JSON.** Write `$0`, `~80x`, `10-15%`, `->`. The scripts convert/escape (`->`→arrow, `~`→tilde, smart-quotes, etc.). Never put LaTeX in JSON.
- **Tailor by selection & ordering**, not invention: pick and lightly rephrase items from `profile.achievements_bank` to match each target role's keywords.
